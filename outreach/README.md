# Helpful outreach

Track real questions where Dave can give a useful, specific answer. The answer must stand on its own without a link. Link only when a Multilingualizer guide, product page or Weglot resource materially helps with the exact question.

The default workflow is semi-automated:

1. Discover and record a relevant question.
2. Check the site and product facts.
3. Draft a unique answer in Dave's conversational register.
4. Get human approval while the channel and acceptable cadence are being established.
5. Post from Dave's real account, disclose the relationship where relevant, and record the URL.
6. Recheck replies and referral/conversion data.

Do not automate accounts, evade moderation, recycle answers, pose as a user, or post solely for backlinks.
