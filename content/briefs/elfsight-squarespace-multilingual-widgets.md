# Problem

# Evidence

- Elfsight was detected for 17 of 674 customers with a reachable site (2.5%), including 14 of 337 customers with a reachable Squarespace site (4.2%).
- Verified audit: 12 review-widget instances across eight customers and seven chat-widget instances across six customers.
- Screenshot evidence and counting method: `data/customer-intelligence/elfsight-review/README.md`.
