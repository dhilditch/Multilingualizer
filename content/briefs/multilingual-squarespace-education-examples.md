# Problem

Education providers need course descriptions, dates, fees, application steps and learner enquiries to remain clear across languages.

# Evidence

- Education and training is the largest classified Squarespace sector in the current dataset: 59 sites overall.
- Multilingualizer is publicly detectable on 38 current Squarespace sites in this sector, belonging to 34 customers.

# Existing URL decision

- Canonical URL: `/multilingual-squarespace-education-examples/`.
- Competing internal URLs: none.
- Keep CRM form setup in the HubSpot guide rather than repeating it.

# Solution structure

1. Verify three to five schools, course providers or training organisations.
2. Check course pages, dates, pricing or application details in every stated language.
3. Review enquiry forms, downloadable documents and any external learning platform.
4. Summarise patterns for local bilingual provision versus international recruitment.

# Product fit and CTA

- Multilingualizer for controlled Squarespace course and information pages.
- HubSpot only where the provider has a genuine admissions or sales pipeline.
- Weglot where the content volume and managed translation requirements justify it.

# Publication checks

- [ ] Verify current course details rather than quoting stale crawl text
- [ ] Check forms and documents as well as page headings
- [ ] No enrolment, revenue or satisfaction claim
- [ ] Offer a factual correction route
