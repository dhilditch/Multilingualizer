# Problem

A store owner must translate the whole buying journey, preserve price and policy accuracy, and know which Squarespace services remain outside Weglot.

# Evidence

- GSC: no query rows in the current export.
- Primary sources: Squarespace Weglot guide and customer notifications, Weglot word-count and dynamic-content guidance, Google international SEO guidance.

# Existing URL decision

- Create `/squarespace-multilingual-ecommerce/`.
- The checkout/forms/emails page answers coverage. This guide owns store planning, catalogue workflow, QA and operations.

# Solution structure

1. Map catalogue and customer journey.
2. Estimate words and choose languages.
3. Configure URLs, translation workflow and notifications.
4. Test products, cart, checkout, tax, shipping, discounts and post-purchase email.
5. Establish an ongoing change process.

# Product fit and CTA

- Weglot is the primary recommendation for a managed commerce journey.
- Multilingualizer callout is limited to compatible on-page manual translation and explicitly excludes protected checkout/account screens.
- Mention Multicurrencyalizer only after its code and live product are revalidated.

# Success metric

Affiliate clicks, calculator completions and ecommerce-assisted conversions.
