# Problem

Food and drink sites need menus, allergens, opening details, reservations and location information to remain usable across languages.

# Evidence

- Eighteen current Squarespace sites were classified as food and drink.
- Multilingualizer is publicly detectable on 14 current Squarespace sites in this sector, belonging to 13 customers.

# Existing URL decision

- Canonical URL: `/multilingual-squarespace-food-restaurant-examples/`.
- Competing internal URLs: none.
- Keep reviews and chat in the Elfsight cluster and link only when the implementation uses them.

# Solution structure

1. Verify three to five restaurants, producers or food businesses.
2. Check menus, prices, allergens, opening hours, maps and reservations in each language.
3. Record which steps remain in Squarespace and which move to a third-party booking system.
4. Summarise repeatable mobile and language-control patterns.

# Product fit and CTA

- Multilingualizer for controlled menus and public Squarespace content.
- Multicurrencyalizer only where the verified display-price use case fits.
- Elfsight only for a specific reviews or chat requirement.

# Publication checks

- [ ] Recheck menus, prices and opening details on publication day
- [ ] Follow the reservation or order hand-off without completing a transaction
- [ ] No sales, booking or satisfaction claim
- [ ] Offer a factual correction route
