# Mission and measurement

## Commercial objective

Increase qualified traffic to multilingualizer.com and turn it into one of three measurable outcomes:

1. A Multilingualizer purchase.
2. A Multicurrencyalizer purchase.
3. A Weglot affiliate click and, where the programme exposes it, a confirmed commission.

Search traffic is an input. Revenue and assisted conversions are the outcome. Content should recommend the right solution rather than force Multilingualizer into cases where a platform's native tools or Weglot are a better fit.

## Audience

- Owners of Squarespace, GoDaddy Websites + Marketing, Webflow, Weebly, Zoho Sites and other hosted-builder sites that need two or more languages.
- Small businesses in bilingual or multilingual markets.
- Designers and agencies implementing multilingual sites for clients.
- Site owners who want manual editorial control or who are comparing a one-off purchase with recurring translation software.
- Store owners who need localised display currencies.

Shopify and Wix need a factual product-fit review before receiving more effort because both platforms have native multilingual features, and the live site contains conflicting statements about Wix support.

## Primary measures

Track rolling 28-day totals and compare them with the preceding 28 days and the same period one year earlier when available:

- organic clicks and impressions from Search Console;
- non-branded organic clicks;
- top-three and top-ten query counts;
- organic landing-page sessions and engaged sessions;
- purchases and revenue by product;
- Weglot affiliate clicks and confirmed commissions;
- organic conversion rate by landing page and product;
- content published, consolidated and refreshed;
- referring domains and referral conversions from helpful outreach.

Record dates of content, template, tracking and product changes. Without annotations, later gains cannot be attributed reliably.

## Guardrails

- Do not chase traffic that has no credible path to one of the three commercial outcomes.
- Do not publish multiple URLs for the same intent.
- Do not state that a builder lacks a feature without checking its current official documentation.
- Do not use legal fear as a conversion tactic. Legal articles must be reviewed against current primary sources and make jurisdiction limits clear.
- Do not claim a sales uplift unless the original study, sample and applicability can be cited.
- Do not report affiliate clicks as sales.
