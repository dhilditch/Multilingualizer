# Affiliate opportunities around multilingual websites

## Commercial rule

The report should solve the visitor's problem before presenting a referral. A relevant owned product normally has better strategic value than a marginal affiliate offer, but it must not be recommended where it is not a technical fit.

## Priority candidates

| Product or programme | Fit | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Weglot | Primary automatic website translation route | Cost estimate, Squarespace buyer guides and full audit report |
| Wise Business partner programme | Complementary international money route | Only for businesses that sell, pay or receive money internationally |
| Squarespace affiliate programme | New-site acquisition rather than existing Squarespace owners | Builder comparisons and visitors who have not chosen a platform |
| WPML affiliate programme | WordPress-specific alternative | WordPress guides where a native plugin is a better fit than Weglot |
| TranslatePress affiliate programme | WordPress-specific visual/manual alternative | WordPress comparisons and manual translation intent |
| Linguise affiliate programme | Weglot competitor across hosted builders and CMSs | Honest alternatives and price comparisons, not stacked beside every Weglot CTA |
| ConveyThis affiliate programme | Weglot competitor across platforms | Test before recommending; use only where its verified fit wins |
| Squarewebsites affiliate programme | Squarespace extensions | Review product quality and relevance before including anything |

## Better owned-product opportunities

The crawl can support products and services with a closer fit than unrelated affiliates:

- a reviewed and renamed Multicurrencyalizer;
- paid Multilingualizer major-version upgrades;
- a migration service from Multilingualizer to Weglot;
- multilingual implementation checks for Squarespace and Webflow;
- translation QA or human-review referrals if a reputable partner is found;
- recurring international SEO monitoring based on Search Console data the site owner explicitly connects.

The audit should select offers from evidence. Currency recommendations require currency or ecommerce signals. WordPress-only products require WordPress detection. Squarespace affiliate links are irrelevant to an established Squarespace site owner.
