# Multilingualizer affiliate register

Checked: 20 August 2026

This folder tracks commercial affiliate opportunities discovered from the public
technology fingerprints on paid Multilingualizer customers' websites. It contains no
customer names, domains, email addresses or other private customer data.

## Files

- `join-queue.md`: programmes worth applying to, in priority order.
- `programme-register.md`: the disposition of every named technology in the current
  customer-intelligence inventory.

## Status definitions

- **Join**: a current, public programme pays cash commission and is open to a publisher,
  creator, consultant or agency matching Multilingualizer.
- **Existing**: a commercially useful programme that Multilingualizer already uses.
- **Consider partner**: cash is available, but the scheme is a reseller, agency,
  ambassador or otherwise gated partner programme rather than a normal affiliate scheme.
- **Ineligible**: a programme exists, but a stated condition currently excludes Dave or
  Multilingualizer.
- **No public programme found**: no current cash-paying programme was found on the
  supplier's official site. This is not proof that no private arrangement exists.
- **Not applicable**: infrastructure, a free public service, an advertising destination,
  or another fingerprint that is not a sensible affiliate product.

## Research rules

- Use official supplier pages as evidence. Network listings and affiliate blogs are not
  sufficient by themselves.
- Record commission figures only when the supplier publishes them.
- Treat affiliate, referral-credit, reseller and technology-partner programmes as
  different things.
- Recheck the official terms immediately before applying or publishing a monetised link.
- Add a clear affiliate disclosure before or beside monetised links.
- Never recommend a weaker product merely because it pays commission. Multilingualizer
  and Multicurrencyalizer sales remain the first commercial objective; affiliate links
  are for cases where another product is genuinely the better fit.

## Scope

The source snapshot contains 834 active HTML sites. Named third-party technology was
found on 726 installations belonging to 600 paid customers. Counts in this folder are
unique paid customers, not script occurrences. Unclassified hosts are excluded until a
deterministic fingerprint identifies the underlying product.
