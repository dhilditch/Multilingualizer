# Repeating growth loop

Run this as a weekly operating loop, with a deeper monthly review.

## Weekly

1. **Collect**: run the GSC, GA4, WordPress and crawl collectors.
2. **Validate tracking**: check purchase and affiliate-click events before interpreting conversion changes.
3. **Triage**: review striking-distance queries, CTR opportunities, declining pages, cannibalisation and indexation problems.
4. **Choose one measurable intervention**: refresh, consolidate, improve a title/snippet, add internal links, create a missing bottom-of-funnel page, or fix a technical issue.
5. **Brief**: record the query, intent, evidence, internal competitors, CTA and expected movement in `content/briefs/`.
6. **Draft and verify**: use Dave's article/KB voice, current primary sources, product facts and screenshots or tested steps where the guide needs them.
7. **Publish deliberately**: preserve good URLs, add redirects only where consolidation requires them, and annotate the date and exact change.
8. **Distribute helpfully**: answer relevant existing questions, then record the thread and any link in `outreach/tracking.csv`.
9. **Measure**: use 7, 28 and 90-day checks. Avoid calling a result after a few days unless it is a tracking or indexing defect.

## Monthly

- Compare organic clicks, landing sessions, purchases, revenue and affiliate clicks with the previous period and prior year.
- Review query-to-page mapping and consolidate competing URLs.
- Refresh price, platform-feature and legal claims.
- Review pages with traffic but no product progression, and pages with conversions but weak visibility.
- Decide whether the next month should prioritise existing winners, a new platform cluster, a linkable asset or conversion improvements.
- Record what was tried, what changed and what should stop.

## Opportunity states

Use these values in a future opportunity ledger or issue tracker:

- `discovered`
- `validated`
- `briefed`
- `drafted`
- `published`
- `measuring`
- `won`
- `lost`
- `superseded`

An opportunity is not `won` because a page was published. It is won when its agreed search or commercial measure improves for a sustained period.

## Publishing gate

Before a draft goes live, confirm:

- the target intent does not have a stronger existing URL;
- platform, pricing, product and legal facts are current and cited;
- the article solves the stated problem step by step;
- title, description, H1, canonical, schema and social image are set;
- it has links from its parent hub and at least two relevant existing pages where natural;
- its CTA matches the reader's platform and problem;
- tracking distinguishes Multilingualizer, Multicurrencyalizer and Weglot actions;
- the final copy contains no em dashes, invented numbers or vague claims.
